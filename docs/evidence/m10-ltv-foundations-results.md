Generated by `python scripts/generate_m10_evidence.py`; do not edit by hand. Seeded; every number reproduces exactly with the versions below (the runtime table excepted). Narrative and limitations: [m10-ltv-foundations.md](m10-ltv-foundations.md); methodology: [../methodology/customer-economics.md](../methodology/customer-economics.md).

Three quantities are kept apart: **realised** (historical) contribution, **projected** contribution (finite horizon) and **expected lifetime** contribution (infinite horizon). All values are discounted at 10% a year unless stated, in fictional currency units, on synthetic data; the truth is read only after estimation.

**Disclosures.**

1. *Protocol error found before evaluation:* the backtest cutoff 36 cannot have a complete 12-month window (it needs month 48; the data end at month 47). The protocol was not edited; the code refuses incomplete windows and §10 reports the cutoff as excluded.
2. *Plan change:* the M3 Weibull survival function is not a reference for this generator (geometric churn with Gamma heterogeneity); the truth is checked against the Gamma Laplace transform (numerical integration) and Monte Carlo instead.
3. *Designed-in mechanisms (not discoveries):* the premium segment has the highest price but a high cost-to-serve; the cheapest channel per acquisition (social) brings high-churn, low-value customers.
4. *Corrections before any main-seed result* (development seeds and tests): the customer backtest compared projections and realised values on different customer sets (fixed: one comparison set); the survivors-only counterexample mixed survivorship and truncation (redefined as complete-window survivors); Kaplan–Meier computed 0 × ∞ when survival reached 0 (restructured).
5. *Defect found from main-seed dry-run output:* Kaplan–Meier censored customers still active at the cutoff at their number of paid months t instead of t - 1 (the churn decision after month t is not yet observed), biasing survival upwards (the dry run's Greenwood intervals contained the truth in 72% of cells). Fixed with a regression test and every result regenerated; it is a correctness fix, not a choice among alternatives, and no other setting was changed after the main-seed output was seen.
