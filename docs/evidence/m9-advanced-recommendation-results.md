**Changes after the protocol was written, all before any main-seed validation or test result was seen** (each prompted by a development-seed diagnostic, never by a validation or test score):

1. Added to the protocol before any evaluation: the paired-test reference model, the fallback rule, and how the four ranker cells are tuned.
2. DR click model fitted **out of sample** (scorer at an earlier cutoff, labels in the next window): the first, in-sample version learned that previously clicked items have high scores and predicted near-zero clicks for the unseen items lists contain, making DR collapse to IPS.
3. LambdaRank pair weights rescaled by one constant (clicked items average 1) — scale only — after the propensity-weighted variant's training NDCG oscillated at the frozen step size; a training-NDCG trace replaced the (non-fixed) loss as the monitor.
4. Pointwise ranker solver tolerances tightened (L-BFGS stopped early; found by the comparison with the M3 logistic regression).
